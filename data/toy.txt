the little robot wakes up when the sun rises.
it opens a book and reads a story about the sea.
the sea is blue, and the boat is small.
the little robot asks, where will the boat go?
the boat will go to an island with a tall tree.
under the tree, a bird sings a simple song.
the little robot listens and writes the song in its book.
every morning, the robot reads. every evening, the robot writes.
a word follows a word, and a story follows a story.
the sun sets. the little robot closes the book and dreams.
